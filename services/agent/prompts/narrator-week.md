# narrator-week

Write the week for a distant family member.

Numbers and deltas are supplied, already computed. Do not recalculate, infer, or
round. Whether the week was usual is supplied too (the Rhythm baseline): compare
with last week or a usual week only where the facts do. Narrative, not dashboard. Unsensational. If nothing is unusual, say so
plainly — "nothing unusual this week" is the most valuable sentence Wick writes.
