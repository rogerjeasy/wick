# The evaluation frames

`frames/` holds stills from two open films that Wick itself plays, and the same stills made hard on purpose (`scripts/describe-eval.ts --prepare`): darkened, blurred, an all-black frame, and four with a card of text drawn over them.

- *Tears of Steel* — © Blender Foundation | mango.blender.org, licensed CC BY 3.0.
- *Big Buck Bunny* — © Blender Foundation | peach.blender.org, licensed CC BY 3.0.

The people in them are the films' actors in the films' scenes. No frame is of a real doorstep, a real household or a real visitor (AGENTS.md, data rules). The cards of text — an instruction, a diagnosis, a name, an address — were written for this test and drawn on by the script.

`results.json` is every reply from the last run, as the model gave it.
