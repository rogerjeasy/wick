Connection

disconnected

Stands in for a paired Fire TV — no hardware, no wscat. One click pairs a fresh device with the household and opens the same WebSocket the real app would. Connected as “Mine” in the family app, it is a family member’s own television instead: it shows that member’s home screen, and opens no channel to the resident’s.

What the television shows

No card yet. Trigger a real door event (see docs/PRODUCTION-TESTING-GUIDE.md §2c) or send test telemetry below.

As a family member’s own television, the keyboard is its remote: the arrows move, Enter is OK, Esc is Back.

As the resident’s television, a card is shown as it arrives and one still unanswered is kept: when the card over it is answered, it comes back. That is this page’s simplification of the television’s own queue, which waits for a natural break in a programme; this page has no programme. Reloading the tab keeps its television; “Disconnect” lets it go. Disconnected from the family app, it says so here within about a minute, as the television’s own app does.

As the resident’s television, P is the Play key: while a card is on screen it reads the card aloud, and P again stops it. The small mark in a card’s corner does the same.

On the mantelpiece, a click on the picture is OK: it offers “Leave it” and “Take it down”. The idle screen, where her kept pictures fill a television left untouched, is the television’s only.
