What Wick is

Wick is consumer software that shows information on a television and answers questions from a connected assistant. It is provided by Roger Bavibidila.

What Wick is not

Wick is not a medical device, an emergency service or a safety system. It does not detect falls, monitor vital signs or diagnose anything, and it makes no clinical claims. Do not rely on it in an emergency. Keep a personal alarm and emergency arrangements independently of Wick.

Consent

Wick may only be used with the genuine, informed and revocable consent of the person whose home it is. It is not a tool for monitoring an adult who has not agreed to it. Accounts found to be used without her consent will be closed.

Connected services

Wick works with Ring, Alexa+ and Amazon Fire TV. Their own terms govern those services, and you must be entitled to connect the accounts and devices you connect.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use Wick to watch, pressure or control another person.
  • Do not try to obtain information she has chosen not to share.
  • Do not resell or redistribute the service without permission.

Availability

Wick depends on third-party services and on the home's internet connection, and may be unavailable. No uptime is guaranteed.

Liability

Wick is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, to the fullest extent permitted by law. The operator is not liable for any loss arising from use of, or inability to use, the service.

Ending use

Anyone may stop using Wick at any time. Closing a home is immediate and deletes everything Wick holds about it, as the privacy policy describes.
