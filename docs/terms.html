Wick — Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 September 2026

What Wick is

Wick is consumer software that surfaces information on a television and answers questions from a connected voice assistant. It is provided by Roger Bavibidila.

What Wick is not

Wick is not a medical device, an emergency service, or a safety system. It does not detect falls, does not monitor vital signs, does not diagnose, and makes no clinical claims. It must not be relied upon in an emergency. Households should maintain an appropriate personal alarm and emergency arrangements independently of Wick.

Consent

Wick may only be used with the genuine, informed and revocable consent of the person whose home it is. It is not a tool for monitoring an adult who has not agreed to it. Accounts found to be operating without resident consent will be terminated.

Connected services

Wick integrates with Ring, Bee, Alexa+ and Amazon Fire TV. Use of those services is governed by their own terms, and you must be entitled to connect the accounts and devices you connect.
