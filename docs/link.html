Connect your Ring account

Wick is asking to read events from your Ring doorbell and chime.

What Wick will be able to do

  • See when the doorbell is pressed or the camera detects a person, so it can put a card on the television.
  • Fetch a still image from the doorbell at that moment, to describe who is there. Images are discarded within 24 hours.
  • Open a live view on the television, only when someone presses “Watch live”.
  • Play a short recorded message through your Ring Chime, when someone presses “Just a moment”.
  • Read the history of door events, to confirm whether an expected visitor actually arrived.

What Wick will never do

  • Stream any camera inside the home to anyone.
  • Record or store video.
  • Share what it sees with anyone the resident has not approved.

Wick is a private integration, used only with accounts explicitly connected to it. You can disconnect at any time from the Ring app, and separately ask Wick to delete everything it holds — a single action, with no negotiation. See the privacy policy and terms.
