Ring account

Connect your Ring doorbell.

Wick asks to read events from the household's Ring doorbell and chime, so a visitor at the door can appear on her television. Here is exactly what that allows, and what it never will.

What Wick will be able to do

  • Know when someone is at the door, from a button press or movement, and put a card on the television.
  • Fetch a picture from that moment to describe who is there. Pictures are deleted within 24 hours.
  • Show the doorbell camera live on the television, only when she presses Watch live.
  • Ring the hallway chime when she presses Ring the hallway chime, in Wick's own chime slot, and never while the chime is snoozed.
  • Read the door's history, to confirm whether an expected visitor arrived.

What Wick will never do

  • Stream any camera inside the home, to anyone.
  • Record or store video.
  • Show the door to anyone she has not allowed.
  • Put a Ring credential on the television or in the family app. Each home's tokens are kept server-side, in a secret of their own.

Wick is a private integration, used only with accounts explicitly connected to it. You can disconnect at any time from the Ring app or the family app, and closing the home in the family app deletes everything Wick holds about it. See the privacy policy and the terms.
